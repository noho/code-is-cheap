### F1 detail (highest-risk item)

The initial plan (`evidence/plan-initial.md`:52, 119-120, hash `918ee363…` confirmed) required only `-----BEGIN CERTIFICATE-----` blocks with equal delimiter counts — the exact ordinary-label-only, count-based contract the accepted finding faulted.
