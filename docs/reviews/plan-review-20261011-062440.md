## Findings

### F3-部分修复-[中]-unsupported notice can be downgraded through anomaly adjudication

- **位置**: `plan.md` "Actual pass assertions", especially lines 238-246; `plan-fix-01.md` F3 disposition.
- **问题类型**: 契约缺失 / 测试缺口 / validation gate漏洞.
- **当前写法**: The plan accepts either `passed` or `needs_review` after root inspects diagnostics and adjudicates every anomaly as compatible. It says errors/rejected fail, but does not state that a runtime-emitted unsupported notice is mechanically non-adjudicable and must remain rejected.
- **反例/失败场景**: Runtime emits an unsupported notice that the current collector records as an anomaly while exits, terminal and final answer still look successful. `agent-run-result.py:218-239,398-402` can therefore produce `needs_review`, not `rejected`. Under the current wording, root could classify that notice as compatible and accept the gate.
- **为什么有问题**: This conflicts with the binding requirement to preserve mechanical rejection for unsupported notices and to avoid broad error downgrade. It can create a false accepted-plan/live-gate result even though the runtime behavior is explicitly unsupported.
- **直接证据**: Plan lines 241-246 permit diagnostic adjudication of remaining anomalies; `agent-run-result.py:398-402` maps anomalies to `needs_review`; no reviewed plan clause names unsupported notices as permanently rejecting.
- **影响**: Unsupported behavior can be hidden behind a successful final/terminal; implementation may omit a necessary assertion; later review cannot distinguish a valid startup from a mechanically unsupported recovery.
- **建议改法和验证点**: Add an explicit non-downgrade rule: any runtime-emitted unsupported notice remains gate-rejected regardless of exit/terminal/final or `validation_status=needs_review`; only narrowly enumerated benign anomalies may be adjudicated. Preserve collector behavior and proof schemas. Add a focused live assertion or root gate check proving the notice cannot be accepted, without broadening ordinary anomaly handling.
- **修复风险（低/中/高）**: 低.
- **严重程度（低/中/高/严重）**: 中.
